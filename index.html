Trips

October 9, 2026 · Central Nevada

First real trail day

Six trails in one day across the high desert of central Nevada. The longest run, Trails 3 through 5, covers about 11 miles from a sagebrush ridge down to a side spur and then south into a canyon.

  • Trail 3: 7.5 miles of ridge-top two-track to the junction
  • Trail 4: a short out-and-back spur, under half a mile
  • Trail 5: 3.4 miles south into the canyon to the turnaround
  • Trail 1 is narrow enough that it needed a ground guide
Coming 2027

Arizona Peace Trail

A roughly 675-mile loop of mostly BLM backcountry through western Arizona. The plan is to drive it in daily sections over a few days, stopping in towns like Oatman, Lake Havasu City and Quartzsite. Trip report to follow.
