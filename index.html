What we stand for

A Nevada backcountry that stays open, known and cared for, so every generation can discover it for themselves.

1

Community, not competition.

Connecting trails and the people who use them. Add to what's there and credit the stewards who came before us.

2

Show up for the land.

Not just treading lightly but actively stewarding. Join cleanups, comment periods and volunteer days, report washouts, closures and dumps, and back the clubs already doing the work.

3

Leave a true map.

Not just where the trail goes, but what we learned as we navigated it. Share honestly how hard it is, and what we know we don't know.

The Rig
